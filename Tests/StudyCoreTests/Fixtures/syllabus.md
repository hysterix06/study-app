# HM210 Revenue Management — Course Outline

## Learning outcomes

Students will be able to forecast demand, set room rates using yield principles, and evaluate distribution channels.

## Assessment

| Component | Weight | Due |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing report (individual) | 30% | 23 October, 23:59 |
| Case study 2 (group of 3) | 20% | 30 October |
| Final exam (minimum 40% required) | 50% | Exam week |

## Weekly plan

Week 1: Introduction to revenue management. Week 2: Forecasting. Week 3: Overbooking and yield.
